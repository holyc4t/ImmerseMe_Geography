# Changelog

From 1.0.0 on, every release is listed on this repository's Releases page, with notes taken from
its pull request. The `CHANGELOG.md` inside each release zip has the full history.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-17

First release.

- A land orb on the upper-left of the minimap shows the terrain, painted in the zone's own colours.
- Hover it (or the zone name) for the ground underfoot, the water and how wild the land is.
- Ores, herbs, game and fish are learned by gathering them, per character, with a reading that
  grows more exact with skill and practice, and a personal record of what you've gathered.
- At high skill, a hint when a zone holds riches you haven't found yet.
- Every classic zone, plus the dungeons and mines where gathering happens.
- `/img style row` shows four small land icons under the zone name instead of the orb.
- Commands: `/img` (or `/geography`); `/img help` lists them all.
